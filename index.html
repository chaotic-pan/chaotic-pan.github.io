About Me

Hello World, I am Liz! I studied International Media and Computing at the University for Applied Sciences Berlin (HTW Berlin), where I received my Master of Science in Internation Media And Computing.
During my studies, I quickly noticed my passion for game development, as well as 3D design and animation. You can find some examples of my work and previous projects below.

  • Distant Beacon

    October 2022 - February 2023
    As part of my studies, I had the opportunity to go abroad for one semester to participate in an international internship.
    Distant Beacon is a software company located in Oulu, Finland, which specializes in game development. While they also offer contracting work for web and software development, as well as audio engineering, I was exclusively part of the game programming team, working on their yet unannounced project.   • JuHu Berlin - Junge Humanisten*innen Berlin

    2021 - present
    JuHu is a humanistic youth association, which organizes a variety of summer camps, workshops or demonstrations, with an emphasis on equality and diversity. (e.g. disability rights, lgbtq+ support, anti-racism, anti-sexism).
    After having been a participant in these summer camps for many years myself, I started working here in 2021 on a voluntary basis, to help with organizing and leading some of these camps and workshops.
    Since 2023, I’ve also taken over responsibilities as a team leader for their annual international exchange camp, which in the past had been in cooperation with youth groups from Finland, Belgium, Iceland, and the Ukraine. Among the tasks I complete as a team leader is organisation and planning before and after the camp, writing of financing applications to EU youth exchanges programs, managing finances on the camp for food, materials, accommodations, etc., as well as leading the team meetings and work distributions.
